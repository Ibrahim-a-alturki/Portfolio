# 🤖 Factors Affecting the Intention to Use AI in Mobile Banking

> Graduation Project (BSBA, Management Information Systems) — a quantitative study on what drives customers to trust and adopt AI-powered features in mobile banking.

**Prince Sattam Bin Abdulaziz University** · College of Business Administration · 2024–2025

---

## 📌 Overview

Banks are racing to add AI features (chatbots, automated financial advice, fraud detection) to their mobile apps — but many customers still hesitate to use them. This study surveyed 100 users to identify **why**, testing four factors: **Trust, User Satisfaction, Perceived Ease of Use,** and **Use Behavior**.

**Research Questions:**
- What factors affect the intention to use AI in mobile banking?
- To what extent does each factor influence adoption, from the user's perspective?

---

## 🔑 Key Findings

| Factor | Contribution to Adoption Intention |
|---|---|
| 🔒 **Trust in Security** | **40%** — the single biggest driver |
| 🖱️ **User Experience** | 35% |
| 💡 **Perceived Benefits** | 25% |

**Other notable results:**
- Trust in security showed the strongest correlation with intention to use (r = .82)
- Younger (20–30) and more educated (postgraduate) users showed the highest adoption intention
- Male participants reported higher trust in AI's data protection than female participants (72% vs. 58%)

---

## 💡 Recommendation

Building strong trust should be the top priority for banks. Transparency, robust security, and consistent service quality matter more to adoption than any single AI feature — banks that get trust right will see the biggest jump in usage.

---

## 🧪 Methodology

- Quantitative, descriptive research design
- Data collected via structured questionnaire (100 participants)
- Statistical analysis: descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation, Chi-square tests of independence
